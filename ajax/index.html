One hub. Every risk.

Ajax Systems builds a wireless security and smart-home system in Kyiv: 280 devices that guard against intrusion, fire, flood and outages, all reporting to one Hub in one app.

Tour the system

Scale model, not to scale: devices are enlarged so you can see them.

The Hub

Hub 2 Plus Jeweller is the control panel the whole house reports to.

  • 1Hub 2 Plus JewellerUp to 200 devices and 200 users · Ethernet, Wi-Fi and two SIMs (LTE/3G/2G) · backup battery for up to 15 hours

Intrusion

Four devices watch the doors, the rooms and the street.

  • 2MotionCamSees motion up to 12 m and takes photos to verify the alarm; ignores pets
  • 3DoorProtectOpening detector for a door or window; up to 7 years on batteries
  • 4KeyPad TouchScreenArm and disarm by touch, code, Tag or Pass; duress codes
  • 5StreetSirenUp to 113 dB with a red LED frame; up to 5 years on batteries

Fire

Kitchen and bedroom: where fires most often start and where they're noticed last.

  • 6FireProtectA photoelectric smoke chamber plus a temperature-rise sensor; up to 3 years on two CR2 batteries

Water

A puck on the floor notices water, and a valve shuts it off before it reaches the neighbours.

  • 7LeaksProtectLeak detector under the bath and the sink; up to 5 years on batteries
  • 8WaterStopMotorised valve: shuts off the water within 7 s of a leak alarm

Comfort

The same radio runs the lights and the power: scenes, schedules and "someone's home" simulation.

  • 9LightSwitchTouch switch for 5–600 W, up to 0.2 W on standby; Red Dot winner
  • 10SocketSmart plug with energy monitoring, under 1 W on standby

Video

Cameras watch the outside, and their events land in the same app as the alarms.

  • 11BulletCamOutdoor 5 or 8 MP camera, IR up to 50 m; Red Dot winner
  • 12TurretCam5 or 8 MP camera with AI object detection

Jeweller

Ajax's own radio protocol ties it all together. Alarms reach the Hub in under 0.15 s from up to 2,000 m away in open space, with block encryption and a dynamic key.

  • 13ReX 2Range extender for the shed, the garage or the next building; up to 5 per system
